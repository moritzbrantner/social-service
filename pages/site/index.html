social-service

What the concept is meant to communicate

The Pages demo visualizes consumer experiences while keeping every authority boundary in the Rust/PostgreSQL service.

01

One social authority

Profiles, post audiences, relationships, groups, chat, and moderation stay authoritative in the service.

02

Many consumers

Next.js, Expo, desktop apps, or games can build their own UX over the framework-independent TypeScript client.

03

Current policy on every read

Timeline, comments, reactions, votes, saves, messages, and pins reapply visibility and safety rules instead of trusting stale client state.
